# Privacy Policy — ImageToPDF

**Last updated:** October 2026

ImageToPDF is an offline-first Android app. Your documents are processed and stored on your device unless you explicitly share or export them.

## Data we collect

- **Account screen (optional):** Email and a local passcode you choose are stored on your device only. There is no cloud account or server login.
- **PDFs and images:** Saved in the app’s private storage (`PDFMaker` folder) and optionally in `Downloads/PDFMaker` when you use Download or enable that setting.
- **Camera:** Used only when you scan documents. Images are not sent to our servers.

## Data we do not collect

- No analytics SDK is bundled in the open-source build.
- No document content is uploaded by the app itself.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|------------|-----|
| Camera | Document scanning |
| Storage (legacy Android) | Reading PDFs you pick on older devices |

## Your choices

- Delete files in **My Files** or clear app data in system settings.
- Turn off “Also save to Downloads” in Settings.
- Log out from Settings to return to the unlock screen.

## Contact

Use **Settings → Help & feedback** in the app or open an issue on the project repository.
