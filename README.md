# ImageToPDF

Turn photos into PDFs, tidy up documents, and handle everyday PDF tasks from your phone. Everything runs on your device — your files stay with you unless you choose to share them.

## What’s inside

**Turn pictures into a PDF**  
Pick images from your gallery, put them in order, and save one PDF.

**Scan a document**  
Use the camera to capture a page, adjust the corners, and save it as a PDF.

**Shrink a PDF**  
Make a large file smaller when you need to send it or save space.

**Password-protect a PDF**  
Add a password so only people with the code can open the file.

**Combine or split PDFs**  
Merge several files into one, or split one file into separate pages or ranges.

**Reorder pages**  
Move pages around and save a new version.

**Edit, highlight, and sign**  
Add text, mark important bits, or draw your signature on a page.

**Convert PDF to images or Word**  
Export pages as pictures, or get an editable document — layout may not match the original perfectly, especially for complex PDFs.

**Ready-made templates**  
Fill in details for things like a resume, invoice, or letter and generate a PDF.

**View PDFs**  
Open and flip through pages inside the app.

**My files**  
See what you’ve created, search and sort, import a PDF you already have, or start from a blank page.

## Getting started

Install the app on your Android phone. The first time you open it, you’ll sign in with email and password (stored on your device). After that you land on the home screen with quick actions and your recent PDFs.

The app may ask to use your camera when you scan documents. That’s only for scanning — not for anything else in the background.

## Good to know

- Your PDFs are stored on your phone. There’s no online account that holds your documents for you.
- Signing in keeps you in the app between visits; you can sign out anytime from Settings.
- Very large PDFs can take a moment on slower phones — that’s normal.
- Converting to Word works best on simple, text-heavy PDFs. Fancy layouts and lots of images won’t copy over exactly.
- If a protected PDF doesn’t open somewhere else, try opening it in another PDF app — protection types vary.

## Privacy

ImageToPDF is built for personal use. Don’t put sensitive information in the app unless you’re comfortable keeping it on your device. Use share and save the way you would with any file on your phone.

## Build from source

1. Install [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) (or the Android SDK) and JDK 17.
2. Copy `local.properties.example` to `local.properties` and set `sdk.dir` to your SDK path.
3. From the project root: `./gradlew assembleDebug`
4. Install the debug APK from `app/build/outputs/apk/debug/` on a device, or run from Android Studio.
